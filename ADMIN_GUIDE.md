# ArtsFlow OS — Administrator User Guide

This guide walks academy directors, school managers, and administrative staff through the operational capabilities of **ArtsFlow OS**.

---

## 1. Organisation Setup & Profile
- **Organisation Profile** (`/settings/organisation`): Configure legal name, academy registration number, physical address, contact telephone, and primary email.
- **Operational Calendar** (`/settings/calendar`): Configure operational terms, semesters, and public holiday blackout dates.
- **Branding & Assets** (`/settings/branding`): Upload academy crest/logo, configure invoice letterhead, and customize theme colors.

---

## 2. Users, Staff & Role Management
- **Users & Roles** (`/settings/users`): Invite new staff members by email, assigning appropriate roles (`organisation_admin`, `programme_director`, `teacher`, `finance`, `viewer`).
- **Staff Directory** (`/staff`): Maintain teacher profiles, instruments/styles taught, employment types (full-time, sessional, contractor), and contact information.
- **Staff Operations** (`/staff-operations`): Manage lesson schedules, track teacher availability, review monthly timesheets, and handle teacher substitutions with transparent attribution.

---

## 3. Programmes, Classes & Enrolments
- **Programmes** (`/programmes`): Define overarching disciplines (e.g., Music, Dance, Drama, Visual Arts).
- **Groups & Classes** (`/groups`): Create specific ensembles, technique classes, or orchestra sections with designated classroom venues and capacity limits.
- **Enrolment Management** (`/enrolments`): Enrol learners into classes with tracked start dates and statuses (`active`, `paused`, `completed`).

---

## 4. Student & Guardian Directory
- **Learners** (`/learners`): Student profile management including medical notes, special dietary/educational needs, and emergency contact details.
- **Guardians** (`/guardians`): Centralized record of parents and sponsors.
- **Linking Relationships**: Map multiple guardians to a learner with designated relationship types (Parent, Grandparent, Sponsor) and primary contact flags.
- **Portal Invitation**: Send cryptographic self-service portal access invites directly from the student's profile.

---

## 5. Teaching & Attendance Operations
- **Session Timetable** (`/sessions`): Schedule individual lessons, group classes, and ensemble rehearsals.
- **Attendance Registers** (`/attendance`): Record attendance (`Present`, `Late` with minutes, `Absent`, `Excused`).
- **Absence Follow-Ups** (`/follow-ups`): Automated tasks generated for students with consecutive unexcused absences.

---

## 6. Music & Dance Specialist Workspaces
- **Music Workspace** (`/music`): Manage instrument asset serials, check-out/return allocations, score repertoire library, and graded musical assessments.
- **Dance Workspace** (`/dance`): Manage dance syllabus levels, choreography scene breakdowns, music cueing, costume inventory sizes, and performance assessments.

---

## 7. Event Production, Consent & Transport
- **Concert & Event Production** (`/events`): Schedule recitals, competitions, and tours with run sheets, staging notes, and participant rosters.
- **Digital Consent** (`/consent`): Issue digital consent forms with legal indemnity waivers for parents to sign online.
- **Transport Logistics** (`/transport`): Assign academy buses or third-party transport providers, track seat capacity, and manage real-time passenger manifests.

---

## 8. Invoicing, Payments & Fee Collection
- **Fee Schedules** (`/finance/charges`): Create recurring tuition charges, instrument rental fees, and event fees.
- **Billing Accounts** (`/finance/invoices`): Issue sequential invoices to guardians with due dates and itemized breakdowns.
- **Payment Recording** (`/finance/payments`): Record Cash, EFT, or Card payments and allocate against outstanding invoices.
- **Receipts**: Generate and email official payment receipts.
- **Aged Debtors**: Review overdue balances categorized into Current, 30, 60, 90+ days.

---

## 9. Communications & Document Management
- **Broadcast Messaging** (`/communication`): Compose and dispatch notices to groups, event participants, or guardians via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
- **Document Repository** (`/documents`): Securely store student contracts, medical clearances, and generated certificates.

---

## 10. Automation & Alerts
- **Automation Rules** (`/automation`): Enable event-driven triggers to alert staff when students miss lessons, when invoices fall overdue, or when concert consent forms remain pending.
